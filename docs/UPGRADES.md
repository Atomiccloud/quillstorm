## Tier Definitions

- **S** - Build-defining. Always pick. Multiplicative or game-changing power.
- **A** - Very strong. Consistent, impactful, rarely a bad choice.
- **B** - Solid. Good in the right build or situation. Reasonable pick.
- **C** - Mediocre. Niche use, underwhelming for its rarity, or outclassed.
- **D** - Weak. Barely noticeable effect. Almost never worth picking over alternatives.

## Modifier Mechanics

### Damage
- Base: **10 damage per quill**
- Formula: `baseDamage * (1 + damageModifier + levelBonus)`
- Level bonus: +5% per level (from level 2+), applied automatically via `getModifier('damage')`
- Scales everything: quill hits, burn DPS, thorns reflection, explosion secondary damage

### Fire Rate
- Base: **3 shots/sec**
- Formula: `fireRate * (1 + modifier)` → `delay = 1000 / adjustedRate`
- +100% modifier = 6 shots/sec

### Projectile Count
- Base: **1 quill per shot**
- Formula: `1 + Math.floor(modifier)`
- Each quill in the volley costs 1 from your pool
- Spread angle: 0.15 radians (~8.5 degrees) between quills

### Projectile Size
- Base: **20x6 pixels** (width x height)
- Formula: `sizeMultiplier = 1 + modifier`
- Scales BOTH visual rendering AND physics hitbox
- +0.5 modifier = 30x9 pixels

### Piercing
- Base: **0** (quill dies on first enemy hit)
- Each point = survive one additional hit
- **No per-enemy tracking** — a quill can hit the same enemy multiple times as it passes through
- This is why piercing feels extremely powerful in dense enemy groups

### Bouncing
- Base: **0** (quill dies on wall contact)
- Each point = one wall bounce before death
- Full elastic bounce (coefficient = 1.0)
- Collides with world bounds only

### Critical Hits
- Base crit chance: **5%** (0.05 raw, ~4.8% effective)
- Effective chance (diminishing returns): `rawCrit / (rawCrit + 1)`
  - 0.05 raw = 4.8% effective (base, no upgrades)
  - 0.20 raw = 16.7% effective (base + 1 Vital Points)
  - 0.45 raw = 31.0% effective (base + VP + Deadly Precision)
  - 0.95 raw = 48.7% effective (base + VP + DP + Critical Master)
  - 1.95 raw = 66.1% effective (base + all four crit upgrades)
- Base crit multiplier: **2.5x**
- critDamage modifier adds to multiplier: `2.5 + critDamageModifier`

### Explosion Radius
- Triggers on quill impact when modifier > 0
- Cap: **400px** maximum
- Secondary damage: **50%** of quill damage to all enemies in radius (except primary target)

### Homing
- Search radius: **300px** for nearest enemy
- Turn rate: `angleDiff * strength * 0.1` radians per frame
- Higher strength = faster tracking correction

### Armor (Exponential Saturation + Soft Cap)
- Base formula: `base = 1 - e^(-raw * 1.2)`
- Soft cap: above 50% effective, returns diminish at 85% rate → **max ~92.5%**
- `if base > 0.50: effective = 0.50 + (base - 0.50) * 0.85`
- Damage reduction: `actualDamage = incoming * (1 - effective)`
- Examples:
  - 0.08 raw (Tough Skin) → **9% DR**
  - 0.24 raw (two uncommons) → **25% DR**
  - 0.48 raw (four uncommons) → **44% DR**
  - 1.05 raw (full chain) → **68% DR**
  - 2.00 raw (heavy stacking) → **85% DR**
  - 3.00 raw (extreme) → **90% DR**
  - 6.00+ raw → **~92.5% DR (cap)**

### Evasion (Exponential Saturation + Soft Cap)
- Base formula: `base = 1 - e^(-raw * 0.9)`
- Soft cap: above 40% effective, returns diminish at 75% rate → **max ~85%**
- `if base > 0.40: effective = 0.40 + (base - 0.40) * 0.75`
- Checked BEFORE shields (successful dodge doesn't consume shield charge)
- Examples:
  - 0.08 raw (Quick Reflexes) → **7% dodge**
  - 0.24 raw (two uncommons) → **19% dodge**
  - 0.48 raw (four uncommons) → **35% dodge**
  - 0.88 raw (full chain) → **51% dodge**
  - 2.00 raw (heavy stacking) → **73% dodge**
  - 3.00 raw (extreme) → **80% dodge**
  - 6.00+ raw → **~85% dodge (cap)**

### Shield Charges
- Absorbs 1 hit per charge
- Hard cap: **10 charges**
- Resets to full each wave
- Grants iframes: 400ms base → diminishes at 0.6x per rapid hit → 100ms floor
- Iframe timer resets after 2s without breaks

### Vampirism
- Proc chance: `stacks / (stacks + 20)` (diminishing)
  - 1 stack = **4.8%**, 5 = **20%**, 10 = **33%**, 20 = **50%**
- Heal amount: `8 + (stacks * 3)` (linear)
  - 1 stack = **11 HP**, 5 = **23 HP**, 10 = **38 HP**

### Elemental Proc Chance
- Formula: `(strength * 0.1) / (1 + strength * 0.1)`
  - 2 strength = **17%**, 3 = **23%**, 5 = **33%**, 10 = **50%**
- Fate's Favor: failed procs get a 30% chance to reroll

### Elemental Evolution Tiers
Elements evolve at strength thresholds: **2 (T1), 5 (T2), 8 (T3), 12 (T4)**

**Lightning** — Stun + Chain Arcs:
- T1: 300ms stun + 1 arc (30% hit damage). T2: 400ms, 2 arcs (40%), 20% arc stun. T3: 500ms, 4 arcs (50%), 30% stun. T4: 600ms, 6 arcs (60%), 50% stun.

**Ice** — Slow → Freeze → Shatter:
- T1: 70% slow (chill) 1.5s. T2: Direct freeze 0.8s. T3: Freeze 1.2s + frost aura (40% slow nearby). T4: Shatter on death (25% max HP AoE + chill).

**Fire** — DoT + AoE Spread:
- DPS: `str × 8 × (1 + dmgMod)`, ×1.5 at T2+. Duration 2s, max 10 stacks.
- T2: Death ignites 1 nearby. T3: Burns slow 20%, death ignites 3. T4: Death explosion (radius scales with stacks).

**Poison** — Amplify + Execute:
- Amp: `str × 25%` per stack. Duration 5s, max 10 stacks.
- T2: Stacks grow (+1/2s). T3: Death spreads stacks to 2 nearby. T4: Execute <15% HP (non-boss) + poison cloud.

**Dual-Element Combos** (Epic): Tempest (shock+ice), Wildfire (burn+poison), Frostfire (burn+ice), Venomshock (shock+poison).
**Elemental Convergence** (Legendary, max 3): +3 all elements, 20% chance for secondary proc.

### Prosperity
- Affects chest drop chance (logarithmic curve, caps at ~14%)
- Shifts rarity weights (higher prosperity → more uncommon/rare/epic drops)
- Small crit chance bonus

### Danger Level
- Per level: +12% enemy stats, +3% elite spawn chance, +10% XP
- Score multiplier: `1 + (totalDangerLevel * 0.15)`

### Thorns
- Reflects damage when player is hit
- Formula: `thornsDamage * (1 + damageModifier)` — scales with your damage upgrades

### Knockback
- Applied when quills hit non-boss, non-stunned enemies
- Formula: `force = knockbackModifier * 200 px/s` in hit direction
- Duration: **150ms** push
- Also applied in explosions (pushes enemies away from blast center)
- Skips: bosses, stunned, frozen, burrowed, rolling enemies

### Distance Damage
- Quills deal bonus damage based on travel distance from spawn point
- Formula: `damage *= (1 + modifier * ratio²)` where `ratio = min(distance / 600, 1.0)`
- Quadratic scaling: minimal bonus at short range, ramps up at 300-400px, peaks at 600px
- Applied BEFORE crit (so crit multiplies the distance bonus)
- At max distance (600px) with 0.40 modifier: +40% bonus damage

### Dodge Counter
- On successful evasion, chance to instantly kill the attacker
- Chance based on **highest tier** of evasion upgrade picked (not stacking):
  - Common: 30%, Uncommon: 40%, Rare: 60%, Epic: 80%, Legendary: 90%
- Normal enemies: instant kill
- Elites/Bosses: 25% max HP chunk (scales with damage modifier)

### Companions
- Baby porcupines that fight alongside you using real Quill objects
- Inherit player abilities at efficiency-scaled values (40% base + 3% per companion, max 70%)
- Inherited: damage, fire rate, crit, bouncing, homing, speed, size, elemental, explosion, elite damage
- Excluded: knockback, distance damage, piercing, vampirism
- Fire 20% of player's projectile count per shot (min 1)

### Apotheosis
- Every 5th volley is "empowered" (deterministic, not random)
- Empowered quills: always crit + proc ALL unlocked elemental effects
- Visual: rainbow trail
